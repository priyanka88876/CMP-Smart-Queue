About C.M.P. Degree College

Chaudhary Mahadeo Prasad (C.M.P.) Degree College, established in 1950, is a constituent PG college of the University of Allahabad.

The CMP Smart Queue & Verification Management Platform aims to digitize campus front-desk administration, ensure transparent service delivery and reduce waiting times.
